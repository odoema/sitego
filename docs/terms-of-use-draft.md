## Product scope

SiteGo is intended to help a user review selected local website changes and publish them to a configured Git remote. The user remains responsible for the files, repository, domain, hosting account, and rights to material they publish.

## Safe use

Users should not use SiteGo to publish unlawful content, credentials, personal data without authority, or material that infringes another person's rights. SiteGo's automated checks are limited signals and do not certify that a site is safe, lawful, accessible, or free of secrets.

## Service and third-party providers

GitHub and website hosts are independent services with their own terms, availability, and technical limits. A successful push does not by itself prove that a deployment completed or that every visitor sees the new version.

Do not rely on this draft for liability limits, warranties, termination, payments, refunds, indemnities, or dispute resolution. Those terms require review against the actual service model and applicable law.
