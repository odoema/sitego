# Legal and release readiness

This is a software product checklist, not legal advice. Copyright notices help identify a claimed owner; they do not by themselves register a mark, establish chain of title, or clear rights in third-party material.

- Confirm whether Brian Odoch Emmanuel personally owns the product or whether an entity, employer, collaborator, or contractor has any rights.
- Preserve Git history and contributor records. Obtain written IP assignments from any paid contributor before accepting work intended to be exclusively owned by the product owner.
- Run a dependency and license inventory for the exact production build; ship third-party notices and required source or license materials.
- Ask Uganda-qualified counsel about copyright, company identity, trademark availability/registration for “SiteGo,” consumer terms, privacy/data protection, electronic payments, and cross-border customers.
- Register/secure domains and social handles only after a name/trademark clearance review.
- Publish a verified privacy notice, terms, acceptable-use rules, security contact, support contact, and retention/deletion process before collecting accounts or payments.
- For any future app catalog: require creator identity/contact, declared license, source provenance, secrets scan, malware/static checks, moderation/takedown contact, update history, and a clear install/deploy permission step.
